Yes, five singers on five microphones need a PA. There are two ways to do it.

- **With your sound engineer:** you provide the PA, monitors, a digital mixer (at least {classicMixerChannels} channels for CLASSIC, {goldMixerChannels} for GOLD), wireless handheld microphones in the SM58 class with a spare, and stands. Your engineer stays at the desk for the soundcheck and the whole show.
- **With ours:** our engineer brings the mixer, all microphones, DI boxes, cables and stands. You provide the PA, monitors on separate lines, power, and a place for the desk with a view of the stage.

For CLASSIC and GOLD we recommend our engineer, who already knows the programme. Nobody in the group uses in-ear monitors.
