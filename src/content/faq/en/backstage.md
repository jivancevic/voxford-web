A clean dressing room that locks, with seats, a mirror, power sockets and a toilet, available from the soundcheck until we have packed up. One hot meal for the crew and {parkingSpaces} parking spaces close to the stage. For GOLD we bring a stage piano and a cajón, so we need car access to the entrance nearest the stage. The hospitality rider lists everything.
