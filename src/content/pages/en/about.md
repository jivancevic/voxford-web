## How it started

The five of us come from choir singing, music education and the Dalmatian klapa tradition. The story started in a student flat, and in {foundingYear} we posted our first short a cappella video. The covers of Croatian and international hits that followed went viral, and invitations from other artists came with them: Tonči Huljić took us to Ljubljana and Vienna with his Mediterraneo project, Marko Kutlić brought us on stage at Tvornica kulture, and Dalmatino called us up as surprise guests at Arena Zagreb.

## The sound

Modern a cappella meets klapa, which is part of Croatia's intangible cultural heritage. Blend and harmony come first, and beatbox stays in the background. For bigger concerts a pianist and a cajón player join the five.

## With other artists

We have recorded and sung with Marko Kutlić, Jasna Zlokić and Jakov Jozinović, Damir Kedžo, Jacques Houdek, Ružica Maurus and Igor Drvenkar of Parni valjak, and Bosque released Narancha featuring Voxford in 2025. HRT invited us to its Christmas shows in 2023 and 2025.
