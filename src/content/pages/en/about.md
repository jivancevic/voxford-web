## How it started

The five of us come from choir singing, music education and the Dalmatian klapa tradition. The story started in a student flat, and in {foundingYear} we posted our first short a cappella video. The covers of Croatian and international hits that followed went viral, and invitations from other artists came with them: Tonči Huljić took us to Ljubljana and Vienna with his Mediterraneo project, Marko Kutlić brought us on stage at Tvornica kulture, and Dalmatino called us up as surprise guests at Arena Zagreb.

## The sound

Modern a cappella meets klapa, which is part of Croatia's intangible cultural heritage. Blend and harmony come first, and beatbox stays in the background. For bigger concerts a pianist and a cajón player join us.

## With other artists

We sing on *Ljudi poput nas* by Jasna Zlokić and Jakov Jozinović, which spent {chartWeeks} weeks at number 1 on the Croatian charts, and we sang it with them at its live premiere at Tvornica kulture. We have also recorded and sung with Marko Kutlić, Damir Kedžo, Jacques Houdek and Ružica Maurus, and Bosque released Narancha featuring Voxford in 2025. HRT invited us to its Christmas shows in 2023 and 2025.

## Where we sing

Besides concerts, we sing at conferences, company events, weddings and Advent programmes. At the MAKK 2022 conference we sang medleys between the talks. In 2025 we sang for Državni zavod za statistiku, the Croatian Bureau of Statistics, in Zagreb, and in June 2026 at Memorijal Boris Hanžeković, the athletics meeting in Zagreb, where we sang the anthem and one song.
