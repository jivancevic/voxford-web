## Your guests stay private

We never name private clients, on this site or in our list of references. The celebration stays yours.

## Confirming the date

A private booking is confirmed with a contract and a deposit, paid when you sign. The deposit is not refunded if the celebration is cancelled. Outside the City of Zagreb and Zagreb County, travel and one night's accommodation come on top of the package.
