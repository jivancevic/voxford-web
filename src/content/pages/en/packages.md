## Our sound engineer or yours

POP-UP works with your sound engineer. For CLASSIC and GOLD you can choose: our engineer knows the programme and brings their own mixer, microphones, DI boxes, cables and stands, and works on your PA and monitors. That is why we recommend them. You can also work with your own engineer, who gets the technical rider from us.

## What comes with the offer

With the offer you get a technical rider for the sound engineer and a hospitality rider for the organiser: dressing room, meal, parking and, outside Zagreb, accommodation. Travel costs and accommodation are not part of the package price.
